 The 35 command is the dispense trigger — sent to 0E:C0 within ~40ms of every button press, every time, across all channels. The payload is channel-specific and consistent across sessions.

  Complete channel payload map:

  
```
  ┌─────────┬───────────────────────────┐
  │ Channel │ 35 payload (full 7 bytes) │
  ├─────────┼───────────────────────────┤
  │ CH01    │ 35 00 00 39 18 05 20      │
  ├─────────┼───────────────────────────┤
  │ CH02    │ 35 00 00 10 27 05 20      │
  ├─────────┼───────────────────────────┤
  │ CH13    │ 35 00 00 18 45 05 10      │
  ├─────────┼───────────────────────────┤
  │ CH14    │ 35 00 00 11 07 04 10      │
  ├─────────┼───────────────────────────┤
  │ CH16    │ 35 00 00 85 31 05 10      │
  ├─────────┼───────────────────────────┤
  │ CH17    │ 35 00 00 86 37 05 10      │
  ├─────────┼───────────────────────────┤
  │ CH19    │ 35 00 00 96 44 05 10      │
  ├─────────┼───────────────────────────┤
  │ CH20    │ 35 00 00 06 45 05 10      │
  ├─────────┼───────────────────────────┤
  │ CH30    │ 35 00 00 90 46 05 10      │
  └─────────┴───────────────────────────┘
```


  Bytes 3–4 appear to be motor configuration (coil travel distance/timing), not price — no consistent ratio to yen values. Byte 6 is 20 for channels 1–2 and 10 for 13+, likely indicating which motor driver
  board.

  ---
  Why standalone 35 won't dispense

  The "drink buttons" log (rapid button presses, no actual drops) shows 35 being sent but no 38 00/39 01 completion sequence — the dispenser receives 35 and ignores it.

  The required setup sequence before 35 will trigger a drop:

```

  VM ROOT → 0E:C0  [38 12]              ← "credit/authorize dispense"
  VM ROOT → 0E:C0  [30 1F 06 03 00]     ← dispenser init/query
  0E:C0   → VM ROOT [B0 00 02 1F 06 03 00] ← dispenser ack
      ... (button press) ...
  VM ROOT → 0E:C0  [35 00 00 XX XX XX XX] ← DISPENSE channel X
      ... ~1–3 seconds (motor runs) ...
  VM ROOT → 0E:C0  [38 00]              ← idle
  VM ROOT → 0E:C0  [39 01]              ← done

```

  The 38 12 is the gate. Without it, the dispenser at 0E:C0 is in an unauthorized state and ignores the 35. This explains why a standalone 35 didn't work — the dispenser needs the arm signal first.

  One additional flag: in the "drink buttons" file CH01 shows 35 00 **08** 39 18 05 20 (byte[2]=08) instead of 00. This likely means out-of-stock — the VM sets bit 3 in byte[2] to flag unavailable channels,
   so the dispenser knows not to run even if armed.

  Minimum sequence to trigger a drop:
  1. Send 38 12 to 0E:C0
  2. Send 35 00 00 XX XX XX XX to 0E:C0 with the target channel's payload

  The 30/B0 handshake appears to be part of normal initialization but may not be strictly required for the dispense itself.
