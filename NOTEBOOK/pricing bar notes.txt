button press addressing scheme is like this:

00:29:34:934  ---  B  BTN:1:P 	08:80 - 08:00   0E  01   94
00:29:35:574  ---  B  BTN:1:R 	08:80 - 08:00   0E  01   9C

where P in the note field = Press and R = released
addresses (left address above, the SOURCE address. The reception address is 8:00 which I generally assume is "VM central"):

Row 1:  8:80 - 8:8B
Row 2:  8:8C - 8:97

If this theory is correct, the pricing addressing should look something like:
8:00 - 8:80  for the first row
8:00 - 8:8C  for the second row
8:00 - 8:98  for the third row
